# Surface: Portfolio home (build/index.html)

Mode: Experience. One-page portfolio. Bookers (producers, agencies, venues) and students judge the work, download the CV and get in touch. Layout follows the owner's sketch: hero, six disciplines, About, Portfolio + Download CV, Get in touch, newsletter, footer socials. Brief pins red and peach colours and a modern, elegant, minimal layout. Forms use mailto only. One placeholder photo fills every slot for now.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A single thin red hoop is the only ornament; every photograph hangs from it. Refuses the category default of a dark full-bleed photo with a serif name centred on top.

OWN-WORLD: Peach studio-sweep ground with lighter peach for fields and deep cabaret red for the About section and the footer (oxblood only behind the lightbox). Oxblood ink. One hairline weight (1.5px) governs every rule, frame, input underline and the hoop. Jost (a geometric sans whose O is a true circle) is the only face: light and large for display, regular for body. Buttons are pills (a hoop seen flat). No shadows, cards or boxes.

STORY: The visitor sees the artist suspended in her hoop, learns her six disciplines, reads who she is, browses plates, downloads the CV, and writes to her.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop: a slim bar with the name on the left and the nav plus an outlined "Contact me" pill on the right. Name in large light type at left, with the tagline beneath and Download CV / Get in touch links. A tall portrait on the right, set inside a large red hoop behind it whose apex shows above the portrait, the hoop hanging from a hairline rope that drops from the header rule (the rigging beam). The rope starts at the beam instead of the viewport top so it never cuts through the nav. Mobile: portrait first, then name. With the portrait below the copy, the rope would cross the name and tagline.

FORM: The Lyra Line, list position 4 of 7, seed:65a5b02c3f5e0f3c. Raises: hairline discipline (Design Annual); photographs develop into view once (Darkroom); the hoop is one continuous stroke drawn once (Neon); zero-padded plate index in the lightbox counter, e.g. "02 / 18" (Film). Below the hero, the disciplines and portfolio plates hang from hairline beams, with each portfolio column on one line. Signature interaction: the rope drops and the hoop closes once on load. Motion grammar: one exponential ease-out, opacity plus blur, played once.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
