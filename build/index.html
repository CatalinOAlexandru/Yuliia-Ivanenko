About me

An aerial artist, dancer and performer specialising in aerial hoop and double hoop, with a background in contemporary dance and circus arts.

I hold an HND in Dance Artist from Performing Arts Studio Scotland and am currently training at the National Centre for Circus Arts in London.

Beyond performing, I teach aerial arts and enjoy creating spaces where others can explore movement, build confidence and develop their physical abilities.

Photography is another part of my creative practice, allowing me to explore performance and artistic expression from a different perspective.
